Functional Specification

The 13 Core Analytical Pillars

PEIS operates across thirteen synchronized disciplines to extract, correlate, evaluate, and trace capital project evidence.

PILLAR_01 INGESTION
📥 Heterogeneous Document Ingestion

Ingests structured and unstructured data formats including PDFs, contracts, specifications, CPM schedules, RFIs, daily logs, email correspondence, and cost spreadsheets.

Formats: PDF, XER, XML, MSG, XLSX, CSV
PILLAR_02 CONTRACTUAL
📋 Requirement Identification

Extracts and catalogs contractual obligations, milestone deadlines, strict notice provisions, deliverable specifications, and dispute escalation protocols.

Outputs: Normalized Clause Registry & Deadlines
PILLAR_03 FACTUAL
🔍 Evidence Identification

Isolates timestamped factual statements, site observations, formal submittals, and schedule update transmissions from raw record text.

Provenance: Verbatim Coordinates & Hashes
PILLAR_04 GRAPH
🔀 Evidence Relationships

Maps cross-document associations, linking RFI threads to submittal reviews, change requests, and resultant contract modifications.

Graph: Multilateral Cross-Document Linkages
PILLAR_05 TEMPORAL
📅 Chronology Development

Constructs unified, multi-source master chronologies that reveal the true sequence of project events across all stakeholder perspectives.

Timeline: Timestamp-Anchored Event Sequences
PILLAR_06 ANALYTICAL
⚖️ Contradiction Detection

Identifies conflicting factual assertions between superintendent logs, official owner correspondence, progress meeting minutes, and contractor claims.

Detection: Discrepancy Flagging & Comparison
PILLAR_07 SCHEDULE
📈 Schedule Intelligence

Evaluates critical path movements, float erosion, logic revisions, and activity duration modifications across consecutive schedule baselines.

Logic: Deterministic CPM Forward/Backward Pass
PILLAR_08 SYNTHESIS
📑 Evidence-Supported Findings

Synthesizes verified factual findings with full, unbroken citation to every supporting document, page, and excerpt.

Dossier: Continuous Citation Lineage
PILLAR_09 RISK
🛡️ Risk Identification

Quantifies schedule slippage risk, cost exposure, constructive acceleration potential, and notice non-compliance liabilities.

Metrics: Exposure Severity & Float Erosion
PILLAR_10 DECISION
🎯 Decision Support

Provides program executives and claims professionals with clear, evidence-backed options, audit matrices, and defensible negotiation positions.

Output: Executive Decision Briefings
PILLAR_11 INTEGRITY
🔒 Deterministic Provenance

Enforces cryptographic traceability: every analytical conclusion points to exact source coordinates with evidence-grounded citations.

Security: SHA-256 Fingerprint Pointers
PILLAR_12 LIFECYCLE
🔁 Dependency-Aware Revalidation

Dynamically flags and updates downstream evaluations whenever new documents are ingested or historical evidence is amended.

Revalidation: Automated Invalidation Cascades
PILLAR_13 GOVERNANCE
👤 Human Review & Authority

Enforces human professional oversight across all analytical outputs. PEIS synthesizes facts; human experts maintain decision authority.

Authority: Explicit Expert Approval Gate
Engineered Trust

Deterministic Analysis Combined with Governed AI

Traditional generic AI models frequently hallucinate unsupported narratives. PEIS was engineered from first principles with an entirely different architecture:

  • Deterministic CPM Logic: Schedule computations and critical path models rely on mathematically verifiable algorithms.
  • Strict Extraction Constraints: Natural language processing is restricted to verbatim extraction, normalization, and structured semantic linkage.
  • Traceable Citation Graph: Every extracted entity is permanently bound to its source document hash and bounding coordinates.

Architectural Safeguards

Zero Black-Box Scoring:

Analytical determinations are directly inspectable through explicit rule chains.

Independent Layer Isolation:

Data ingestion, analytical synthesis, and presentation layers maintain strict boundaries.

Immutable Audit Ledger:

All analytical states and revalidation cascades are logged for structured auditability.
