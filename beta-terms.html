Founding Beta Participation Terms

Last Updated: September 2026 (Phase 1 Draft)

1. Program Scope & Eligibility

The PEIS Founding Beta Program is a closed, invitation-only evaluation cohort. Admitted organizations receive temporary, controlled access to evaluate PEIS analytical capabilities on approved project record sets. Admission is determined on an application-by-application basis.

2. Bilateral Non-Disclosure & Data Protection

Prior to any project document ingestion, participating organizations and the Owner shall execute a formal Bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) and Data Processing Schedule. Customer project data remains the exclusive property of the participant and will not be shared, pooled, or utilized for public training.

3. Evaluation-Only Use & Non-Commercial License

Access granted during the beta period is strictly for internal analytical evaluation, benchmark testing, and workflow validation. Beta outputs may not be sold or sublicensed to third parties without prior written consent from the Owner.

4. Structured Feedback Commitments

Participants agree to participate in periodic structured evaluation sessions and provide feedback across analytical categories including chronology accuracy, contradiction utility, schedule intelligence clarity, and workflow time savings.

5. Limitation of Liability

PEIS is provided for evaluation purposes as a pre-commercial analytical release. In no event shall the Owner be liable for direct, indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages resulting from beta participation or reliance upon analytical evaluations.
