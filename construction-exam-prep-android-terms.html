Google Play handles payment, cancellation and refunds under its applicable terms. Cancel automatic renewal using Google Play's subscription controls. Pending, canceled-before-completion, invalid or unverified purchase attempts do not independently create Pro access. Changes between plans take effect according to Google Play's confirmed transaction and subscription status.
