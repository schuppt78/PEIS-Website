Plans and access

The app is free to download. US Pro Monthly is $9.99/month and Pro Annual is $49.99/year. Both renew automatically and provide the same eligible Pro features. Apple returns the actual localized price and billing period before purchase. No advertisements, free trial or new lifetime plan is offered.

Open Free & Pro / Purchase Access and select Load App Store plans. Restore Purchases is on that screen. Apple authenticates purchases and restoration; no separate PEIS account is required for iOS. Manage Apple subscriptions opens Apple's interface. A plan change can be scheduled; the app reports the current verified plan. Cancellation generally retains access through the paid-through date, subject to refunds or revocations. Deleting the app does not cancel billing. Apple handles refunds and payment issues.

Courses and qualification limits

Courses include NASCLA, four Georgia choices, four North Carolina components, Florida General Contractor, Florida Residential and Florida Marine Specialty. Shared Finance is a component. Review all in-app qualification notices before subscribing. Residential trade preparation is restricted to January–July 2027. Shared Finance retains its reviewed 2026 qualification and is not qualified for 2027 examination dates. Marine excludes F4.1, F4.2, F4.3, H3.6 and I4.1. Florida Roofing is unavailable. No licensing or exam-pass guarantee is made.

Progress and diagnostic help

Progress and history are local. Do not delete or reinstall as routine troubleshooting because local progress may be lost. Optional local diagnostics can be shared through the device interface; review their contents and select a recipient. The app does not automatically send those records to PEIS.
