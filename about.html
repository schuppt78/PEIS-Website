Our Mission & Purpose

Engineered for Verifiable Factual Truth in Complex Projects

PEIS Intelligence LLC develops the Project Evaluation Intelligence System (PEIS) to solve one of the most pervasive, costly challenges in modern infrastructure and construction: the fragmentation and distortion of project records during critical evaluations.

MISSION_STATEMENT  |  The Problem We Solve
SYSTEMIC DISPUTE & DELAY OVERHEAD

Why Black-Box Systems Fail High-Stakes Project Governance

In high-stakes capital infrastructure, disputes and management failures rarely occur because of a lack of documentation. They occur because documentation is overwhelmed by volume, scattered across disconnected repositories, and obscured by competing stakeholder narratives.

When a complex delay claim or contract dispute emerges, technical teams spend thousands of hours performing manual keyword searches, cross-referencing daily logs against schedule updates, and attempting to construct a coherent timeline. PEIS eliminates this manual drag by transforming fragmented project records into structured, mathematically verifiable evidence.

Engineering Philosophy

The 4 Foundational Convictions of PEIS

Architectural rules governing how our analytical engine processes records, reasons over timelines, and delivers intelligence.

CONVICTION_01

Evidence Precedence & Provenance

No finding, metric, or timeline event exists without an unbroken, inspectable hyperlink to exact source coordinates (file SHA-256 hash, page, and bounding box). We eliminate orphan assertions and unsupported extrapolations.

CONVICTION_02

Deterministic Mathematical Rigor

Critical calculations—such as Critical Path Method (CPM) float erosion, schedule variance, and contractual notice windows—are computed via deterministic mathematical algorithms, never probabilistic guesses.

CONVICTION_03

Human Review Authority as Sovereign

PEIS is an analytical intelligence platform engineered to augment project executives, forensic engineers, and legal counsel. We never execute unreviewed, autonomous legal determinations or binding project decisions.

CONVICTION_04

Continuous Revalidation Integrity

Project records evolve dynamically. Whenever new daily logs, change orders, or schedule updates are ingested, PEIS executes targeted dependency revalidation cascades across affected downstream findings.
