1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Construction Exam Prep mobile application published by PEIS Intelligence LLC. It explains what information PEIS Intelligence LLC collects through the app and how purchase-related information is handled.

2. Data We Collect

The iOS app does not require a PEIS account. PEIS Intelligence LLC does not operate an iOS receipt-verification, analytics, advertising, or tracking backend for the app.

The app stores diagnostics on your device and lets you choose whether and with whom to share a report. If you contact PEIS for support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send, including diagnostic reports. We use them to respond and troubleshoot.

Support correspondence and attachments are kept in our Microsoft 365 mailbox. The owner of PEIS Intelligence LLC handles support and privacy requests. We do not routinely delete support correspondence. To request deletion, email todd@peisintel.com. Copies may remain recoverable or retained under applicable provider settings or legal obligations after removal from the active mailbox.

3. Purchases and Subscriptions

Paid subscriptions and eligible prior purchases are processed through Apple’s App Store and StoreKit services. Apple may process information associated with your Apple ID, payment method, purchase history, subscription status, and device in accordance with Apple’s own privacy practices and terms.

The iOS app queries Apple’s StoreKit services to determine available products and current entitlements. PEIS Intelligence LLC does not receive, upload, or store Apple purchase receipts or transaction records on a PEIS-operated server for the iOS app.

Subscription management, billing, cancellation, refunds, and payment processing are handled by Apple under the terms applicable to your App Store account.

4. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Construction Exam Prep does not use third-party advertising networks, behavioral advertising, cross-app tracking, or analytics SDKs in the iOS app. PEIS Intelligence LLC does not sell or rent app user data.

5. Data Stored on Your Device

The app may store information locally on your device that is necessary for app operation, such as study progress, practice results, preferences, or local entitlement-related state. This locally stored information is used to provide app functionality and is not transmitted to PEIS Intelligence LLC through the current iOS commerce architecture.

Deleting the app may remove locally stored app data, subject to iOS behavior and any operating-system backup or restore functionality controlled by Apple.

6. Children

Construction Exam Prep is intended as a professional and educational study tool for contractor licensing and construction examinations. PEIS Intelligence LLC does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child sent us personal information, contact us to request deletion.

7. Changes to This Policy

PEIS Intelligence LLC may update this Privacy Policy when the app’s functionality, data practices, or legal requirements change. Material changes will be reflected on this page by updating the effective date. If future app functionality begins collecting data that is not described here, this policy and the applicable app-store privacy disclosures will be updated before that functionality is released.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or Construction Exam Prep privacy practices may be directed to PEIS Intelligence LLC at todd@peisintel.com.

9. Android verification and retention

Android sign-in and subscription verification

Android Pro subscription access uses Google Sign-In, Google Play and a purchase-verification service operated by PEIS Intelligence LLC. Free samples do not require a Pro subscription. When you use Android subscription features, the service processes a Google authentication token and a stable Google account identifier to associate verified purchases with your account. The service stores the Google account identifier, a derived account binding, a hash and encrypted copy of the purchase token, the subscription product, current verification status, expiration and verification times, and acknowledgment/reconciliation information. These records support purchase verification, restoration, fraud prevention and subscription management. We do not receive your payment-card details through this service.

Google provides authentication, payment and purchase-status services. Cloudflare Workers and D1 host our Android verification service and its database. The application communicates with the verification service over HTTPS. The service encrypts stored purchase-token copies; the database also contains account and subscription metadata. It does not use purchase records for advertising or sell them. Service providers process information needed to operate and secure their services under their applicable terms and privacy practices.

The verification service does not intentionally write authentication tokens, purchase tokens, payment details or account identifiers to application diagnostic logs. Stored Worker observability/invocation logs and logpush are disabled for this internal-test deployment. This does not mean providers process no technical network or security information. Optional diagnostics remain your choice to share; do not include passwords or payment information in support messages.

Study progress, practice results and preferences remain on the device under the application's existing storage behavior. The Android verification service does not upload this educational progress. Subscription access requires verified evidence; purchasing, canceling, restoring or deleting developer account data is not inferred from button clicks alone. Connectivity interruptions can make paid access temporarily unavailable.

Retention and deletion of Android verification data

The current verification service has no automatic deletion schedule for account and purchase records. Records remain until an applicable deletion request or authorized removal. You may use the application's account-data deletion control or email todd@peisintel.com. Removing developer account data does not cancel a Google Play subscription or erase Google's transaction records; manage cancellation through Google Play. If you later restore purchases, verification data may be created again as needed to provide the requested service.

Deletion removes the applicable records from the active verification database. Copies can remain temporarily recoverable under provider backup/recovery settings or retained for applicable legal requirements. The configured Cloudflare D1 Free service provides a documented seven-day recovery window. We do not promise immediate erasure of every recoverable provider copy or a fixed permanent-deletion deadline. Support correspondence and voluntarily shared attachments are retained in Microsoft 365 as described elsewhere in this policy; the Owner handles deletion requests at todd@peisintel.com.
