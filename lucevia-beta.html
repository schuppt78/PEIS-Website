Who we are looking for

People who actually review pursuits.

  • Small government-contractor owners or pursuit teams.
  • Capture managers, proposal managers and compliance reviewers.
  • Practitioners able to complete guided tasks and explain disagreements with the results.

You do not need to supply a live bid or proprietary dataset to participate.

What participation involves

Specific tasks. Traceable feedback.

  • Start with supplied fictional company and solicitation materials.
  • Work through evidence review, qualification and an explicit pursuit decision.
  • Review an amendment, prepare forms and inspect a final package.
  • Report blockers, misunderstood states and unnecessary effort; discuss whether the workflow would earn a place in your process.
