Evidence-backed qualification and proposal review.

Lucevia is a standalone Opportunity & Pursuit Intelligence product, not just a bid/no-bid score. Its current evaluation connects these activities in a human-reviewed workflow.

01 / PREPARE

Company evidence & search profiles

Retain company information and supporting records. Save and reuse search profiles across supported public feeds, live APIs and assisted source capture. Lucevia preserves the acquisition source and shows connection status and coverage limitations explicitly.

02 / QUALIFY

Requirement-level coverage

Review each requirement against current evidence, scope and limitations. Distinguish support, partial support, missing evidence and contradictions without inventing positive credit.

03 / DECIDE

Human pursuit decisions

Record BID, NO-BID or HOLD explicitly. Preserve the supporting context and human decision history; an AI suggestion is not permission to submit.

04 / RECONSIDER

Solicitations & amendments

Review source versions, requirements and affected work. Retain existing materials while explicitly reconsidering decisions that need a current evidence basis.

05 / PREPARE

Forms & bid packages

Prepare source-defined form fields, reuse reviewed company facts, and keep unknowns explicit. Attaching an artifact remains separate from determining that an obligation is satisfied.

06 / REVIEW

Compliance & final package

Inspect reviewed-obligation readiness, governed approvals and package identity. Final human authorization remains separate from preparation and review.

Before you request access

Understand the current evaluation scope.

Does Lucevia discover live opportunities from government sources?

Yes, within verified source coverage. The current evaluator candidate supports SAM.gov public-feed discovery, Grants.gov live discovery, USAspending award intelligence and assisted Florida notice capture. Source status and limitations remain visible in the product; the SAM Opportunities API is retained but currently degraded and is not required for SAM public-feed discovery.

Does it automatically certify qualifications or submit bids?

No. Source interpretation, evidence sufficiency, pursuit decisions and submission authorization require human judgment. Internal human-reviewed results do not establish autonomous classifier accuracy, legal compliance or award probability.

What has and has not been validated?

The frozen evaluator candidate has completed controlled internal validation, clean-package extraction, multi-source opportunity acquisition, evidence-backed qualification, human pursuit decisions, proposal/compliance workflow checks and restart persistence. This does not establish production-scale performance, independent customer outcomes, certified translation quality or autonomous classifier accuracy. Human review remains required.

Is Lucevia the same product as PEIS?

No. Both are products of PEIS Intelligence LLC. PEIS focuses on project records and evaluation. Lucevia focuses on opportunity qualification, pursuit decisions and proposal preparation. Their evaluation programs are separate.
