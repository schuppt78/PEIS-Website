1. Information We Collect

PEIS Intelligence LLC develops the Project Evaluation Intelligence System (PEIS). This public website (peisintel.com) collects only information submitted voluntarily through our Founding Beta application form and general inquiry forms. This includes:

  • Contact Information: Full name, business email address, professional job title, and organization/firm name.
  • Project Scoping Metadata: Industry sector, project type, approximate contract scale, estimated document volume, and high-level evaluation challenges.

Confidential Records Notice: We do not collect, request, or store confidential project documents, proprietary contracts, classified data, or Protected Health Information (PHI) through this public website. All formal project document evaluations occur under separate, dedicated bilateral agreements and secure onboarding procedures.

2. How We Use Collected Information

Information submitted through this website is utilized exclusively by PEIS Intelligence LLC to:

  • Evaluate organizational eligibility for the PEIS Founding Beta Program;
  • Communicate directly with applicants regarding beta cohort scheduling and briefings;
  • Respond to technical and commercial inquiries submitted through the contact form;
  • Maintain necessary administrative records in accordance with standard business practices.

3. Information Sharing & Non-Disclosure

PEIS Intelligence LLC does not sell, rent, lease, or monetize applicant contact information or project scoping metadata. Submitted information is shared only with internal personnel directly responsible for assessing beta applications and responding to commercial inquiries.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

This website does not utilize advertising pixels, third-party behavioral trackers, or cross-site tracking technologies. Basic technical session data or privacy-conscious server analytics may be used strictly to ensure website availability, security, and performance.

5. Data Retention & Inquiries

Applicants and visitors may request deletion or modification of their submitted application data at any time by contacting PEIS Intelligence LLC at contact@peisintel.com.
