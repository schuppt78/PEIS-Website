Website Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 (Phase 1 Draft)

1. Information We Collect

During Phase 1, the Project Evaluation Intelligence System (PEIS) public website collects only information submitted voluntarily through our Founding Beta application form and general inquiry forms. This includes:

  • Contact Information: Full name, business email address, professional job title, and organization/firm name.
  • Project Metadata: Industry sector, project type, approximate dollar scale, estimated document volume, and high-level evaluation challenges.

Confidential Records Notice: We do not collect, request, or store confidential project documents, proprietary contracts, classified data, or Protected Health Information (PHI) through this public website.

2. How We Use Collected Information

Information submitted through this website is utilized exclusively to:

  • Evaluate organizational eligibility for the PEIS Founding Beta Program;
  • Communicate directly with applicants regarding beta cohort scheduling;
  • Respond to technical and commercial inquiries submitted through the contact form;
  • Maintain internal records of commercial interest in accordance with regulatory requirements.

3. Information Sharing & Non-Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize applicant contact information or project metadata. Submitted information is shared only with internal evaluation personnel directly responsible for assessing beta applications.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

The Phase 1 website does not utilize invasive advertising pixels, third-party behavioral trackers, or cross-site tracking technologies. Basic essential session cookies or privacy-conscious server analytics may be used strictly to monitor site availability and performance.

5. Data Retention & Inquiries

Applicants may request deletion or modification of their submitted application data at any time by contacting privacy@peisintel.com.
