Functional Catalog

Granular Capability Specifications

Each module provides deterministic processing, structured output schemas, and continuous citation provenance.

MODULE_01 · TEMPORAL ● IMPLEMENTED

Master Chronology Reconstruction

Extracts, normalizes, and sequences timestamped events across contracts, submittals, RFIs, meeting minutes, daily logs, and email correspondence into a unified multi-perspective timeline.

Ingested Records Daily logs, meeting minutes, RFIs, email threads, submittals
Structured Output Multi-party chronological event graph with ISO 8601 timestamps
MODULE_02 · PROVENANCE ● IMPLEMENTED

Evidence Mapping & Graphing

Establishes semantic linkages between contractual covenants and factual execution records, tracking compliance, variations, and document dependencies.

Ingested Records Specifications, prime contract, drawings, inspection reports
Structured Output Clause-to-record relational citation graph with SHA-256 anchors
MODULE_03 · CONTRACTUAL ● IMPLEMENTED

Contract Requirement Evaluation

Systematically evaluates project performance against contractual notice windows, milestone deliverables, and statutory mandates with explicit justifications.

Ingested Records General conditions, special provisions, technical specifications
Structured Output Requirement compliance status matrix (Met, Partial, Disputed)
MODULE_04 · REASONING ● IMPLEMENTED

Contradiction & Discrepancy Analysis

Cross-examines multi-party records to identify irreconcilable factual assertions between superintendent logs, owner letters, payment submittals, and formal claims.

Ingested Records Multi-party correspondence, site inspector logs, contractor submittals
Structured Output Discrepancy inspection dossier with dual-source citations
MODULE_05 · SCOPE ● IMPLEMENTED

Change Event & Modification Analysis

Traces proposed change orders, potential change notices (PCNs), and unilateral directives back to initial field conditions, RFIs, or design bulletins.

Ingested Records Change orders, bulletined revisions, site directives, cost submittals
Structured Output Causal change lineage linking triggering RFI to final modification
MODULE_06 · LATENCY ● IMPLEMENTED

RFI Timeline & Latency Analysis

Evaluates Request for Information (RFI) submission and response intervals against contractual review turnaround limits to identify design clarification latency.

Ingested Records RFI logs, engineering responses, architect submittal tracking
Structured Output Turnaround duration delta and critical-path activity association
MODULE_07 · GOVERNANCE ● IMPLEMENTED

Contract Notice Compliance Analysis

Validates whether formal notices of delay, differing site conditions, or disputed work satisfied contractual delivery windows and notification methods.

Ingested Records Formal delay letters, certified mail receipts, contract notice clauses
Structured Output Notice timeliness classification (Timely, Late, Defective Notice)
MODULE_08 · CPM SCHEDULE ● IMPLEMENTED

Schedule Intelligence & Float Analysis

Executes deterministic CPM schedule analytics across Primavera XER and XML updates to track critical path migration, float consumption, and logic revisions.

Ingested Records Primavera P6 XER baselines and monthly update schedules
Structured Output Critical path evolution, float variance delta, logic alteration log
MODULE_09 · SYNTHESIS ● IMPLEMENTED

Finding & Exposure Synthesis

Aggregates discrete evaluations, CPM schedule calculations, and contradiction records into structured executive findings and exposure scorecards.

Ingested Records Evaluated findings from Modules 01 through 08
Structured Output Evidence-grounded finding summaries and exposure matrices
MODULE_10 · INTEGRITY ● IMPLEMENTED

Deterministic Provenance & Audit Trail

Ensures every analytical statement is permanently bound to exact byte coordinates, page numbers, and SHA-256 cryptographic source fingerprints.

Ingested Records All ingested project records and intermediate analytical states
Structured Output Cryptographic verification package with exact excerpt coordinates
MODULE_11 · LIFECYCLE ● IMPLEMENTED

Controlled Dependency Revalidation

Maintains an active graph of document dependencies, automatically invalidating and updating downstream findings whenever new records are introduced.

Ingested Records Newly uploaded or updated project record revisions
Structured Output Targeted invalidation cascades and incremental re-evaluation log
MODULE_12 · COST (ROADMAP) ○ PLANNED ROADMAP

Cost Ledger & Event Correlation

Direct integration linking cost ledger line items, payroll records, and vendor invoices directly to specific schedule disruption events.

Planned Ingestion ERP cost reports, certified payroll, subcontractor pay applications
Planned Output Direct damages vs delay cost allocation curve (In engineering review)
MODULE_13 · ENTITY (ROADMAP) ○ PLANNED ROADMAP

Personnel & Deposition Cross-Verification

Correlates key personnel statements across meeting minutes, daily logs, emails, and deposition transcripts to map individual knowledge timelines.

Planned Ingestion Deposition transcripts, correspondence, organizational charts
Planned Output Witness timeline reconstruction & testimony cross-verification
