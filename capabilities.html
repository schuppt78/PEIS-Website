Capabilities Matrix

Transparently detailing current operational analytical modules versus planned roadmap capabilities.

● Operational Current in Core Validation Candidate
○ Planned Roadmap Scheduled for Subsequent Releases
📅
Operational

Master Chronology Construction

Extracts and standardizes dates across heterogeneous record types (contracts, submittals, RFIs, meeting minutes, daily logs, email correspondence) to generate a unified multi-perspective timeline of project events.

  • Temporal entity resolution across varying date formats
  • Multi-party viewpoint correlation
  • Filterable chronology queries by party, topic, and timeframe
🗺️
Operational

Evidence Mapping & Graphing

Establishes semantic linkages between contractual requirements and corresponding performance evidence, tracking how project activities meet or deviate from covenants.

  • Clause-to-record relational linking
  • Cryptographic document hash binding
  • Traceability graph traversal
⚖️
Operational

Requirement Evaluation

Systematically assesses project execution against governing contract clauses, specifications, milestones, and statutory mandates.

  • Compliance status determination (Met, Partial, Unmet, Disputed)
  • Verifiable justification trails for each requirement
  • Quantitative requirement coverage metrics
⚡
Operational

Contradiction & Discrepancy Analysis

Scans across conflicting documentation to detect irreconcilable factual assertions between contractors, architects, inspectors, and program owners.

  • Direct contradiction identification with dual-source citations
  • Confidence scoring on factual conflicts
  • Automated conflict escalation for human review
🔄
Operational

Change Event & Modification Analysis

Traces proposed change orders, potential change notices (PCNs), and unilateral modifications back to initial field conditions, RFIs, or design alterations.

  • Causal change chain reconstruction
  • Scope deviation tracking against baseline specifications
  • Cumulative change volume analysis
📬
Operational

RFI Timeline & Responsiveness Analysis

Evaluates Request for Information (RFI) lifecycles against contractual response time limits, isolating delays in design clarifications.

  • Turnaround duration calculations vs contractual windows
  • Critical path activity impact tagging
  • Late response cumulative delay calculations
⏱️
Operational

Notice Compliance Analysis

Validates whether formal notice of delay, differing site conditions, or disputed work complied with contractual notice windows and delivery protocols.

  • Notice deadline calculation from triggering event date
  • Timeliness classification (Timely, Late, Defective Notice)
  • Waiver / forfeiture risk evaluation
📊
Operational

Schedule Intelligence & Float Analysis

Performs deterministic CPM schedule analytics on Primavera XER and XML files to track critical path evolution, float consumption, and logic changes.

  • Critical path migration across schedule updates
  • Float erosion and preferential logic change detection
  • As-Planned vs As-Built variance mapping
📑
Operational

Finding & Risk Synthesis

Aggregates discrete evaluations, schedule calculations, and contradictions into structured executive findings and exposure scorecards.

  • Evidence-grounded finding summaries
  • Schedule and dispute exposure risk scoring
  • Defensible audit trail for executive decision-makers
🔒
Operational

Deterministic Traceability

Ensures every analytical conclusion links directly to an immutable source record with page and paragraph citations.

  • SHA-256 document fingerprinting
  • Zero ungrounded speculative assertions
  • Audit-ready export packages
🔄
Operational

Controlled Dependency Revalidation

Maintains an active dependency graph between documents, requirements, and findings so that modifications trigger targeted re-evaluation.

  • Cascade detection for upstream record alterations
  • Invalidation flags on affected downstream findings
  • Incremental re-evaluation without full reprocessing
💰
Planned Roadmap

Cost / Event Correlation

Deep integration linking cost ledger line items, payroll records, and vendor invoices directly to specific schedule disruption events.

  • Direct damages vs delay cost allocation (Under active development)
  • Earned value disruption curve mapping
👥
Planned Roadmap

Witness & Key Personnel Correlation

Advanced entity mapping correlating key individual participation across meeting minutes, daily logs, emails, and deposition transcripts.

  • Individual knowledge timeline reconstruction (In engineering review)
  • Deposition record cross-verification
