The Evaluation Methodology

The 8-Stage Evaluation Pipeline

PEIS enforces a rigorous, continuous provenance chain connecting every raw source record to executive decision support while preserving full auditability.

01 Ingestion

Source

Contracts, CPM schedules, RFIs, daily logs, submittals & official correspondence.

02 Extraction

Requirement

Contractual clauses, milestone commitments, notice deadlines & technical specifications.

03 Provenance

Evidence

Contemporaneous timestamped records, verbatim excerpts & cryptographic content hashes.

04 Analysis

Evaluation

Deterministic analytical models combined with governed semantic extraction mapping.

05 Inspection

Contradiction

Automated detection of factual discrepancies, notice lapses & conflicting records.

06 Synthesis

Finding

Defensible, evidence-supported factual synthesis with full cross-document citation.

07 Quantification

Risk

Schedule delay slippage, cost exposure & contractual risk quantification.

08 Human Authority

Decision

Human professional review, expert judgment & executive leadership governance.

🔄
Continuous Dependency-Aware Revalidation: Downstream findings, contradiction states, and risk scores automatically re-evaluate whenever source records or evidence are updated.

Trust & Governance

Enterprise Security & Provenance Architecture

PEIS is built from first principles on deterministic computation, cryptographic provenance, and explicit human decision authority.

🔒

Controlled Access & Isolation

Granular role-based and attribute-based access controls (RBAC/ABAC) with strict workspace boundaries preventing data co-mingling.

🔗

Cryptographic Provenance

Every finding, score, and citation maintains a deterministic SHA-256 hash linking back to original source documents and page numbers.

🔄

Continuous Revalidation

Dependency-aware recalculation ensures that new evidence automatically updates downstream evaluations without manual tracking.

👤

Human-in-the-Loop Authority

PEIS provides analytical augmentation and factual synthesis. Professional human experts maintain final evaluation and decision authority.

🛡️

Edge Security Architecture

Static edge distribution with strict Content Security Policies, TLS 1.3 encryption, and zero database query surfaces on presentation tiers.

✅

Internal Test Harness

PEIS has completed internal regression and acceptance testing against its governing validation baseline across core parser, correlation, and provenance modules. Independent external validation is planned under the Founding Beta program.
