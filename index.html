The Core Evaluation Problem

Critical project decisions and risk assessments are routinely fractured across contracts, CPM schedules, RFIs, correspondence, daily logs, submittals, change order modifications, and meeting minutes. PEIS organizes and evaluates these intricate relationships while maintaining deterministic, cryptographically verifiable traceability to every underlying source document.

Platform Capabilities

Engineered for High-Stakes Project Evaluation

PEIS is built specifically for project controls, contract dispute analysis, construction management, and executive oversight.

📂

Heterogeneous Record Ingestion

Ingests contracts, schedules (XER/XML), RFIs, meeting minutes, daily reports, and cost ledgers without loss of context.

⏱️

Master Chronology & Mapping

Constructs precise, multi-source event timelines linking specific contractual requirements directly to factual occurrences.

⚠️

Contradiction & Discrepancy Detection

Automatically flags factual inconsistencies between contractor logs, owner correspondence, and schedule claims.

📊

Schedule DNA & Float Intelligence

Performs deterministic CPM schedule analysis, identifying critical path shifts, float consumption, and logic changes.

🔗

Cryptographic Provenance

Every single finding, evaluation, and score maintains unbroken citation back to exact source page numbers and document hashes.

🔄

Dependency-Aware Revalidation

When new evidence is introduced or source records change, all downstream evaluations and findings are re-evaluated.

Human-in-the-Loop Governance

Analytical Augmentation, Not Autonomous Decision Making

PEIS does not replace project professionals, legal counsel, or program executives. It is purpose-built to empower them with verified factual synthesis, eliminating hundreds of hours of manual document cross-referencing.

  • Zero Autonomous Determinations: PEIS synthesizes evidence; human leaders exercise final judgment.
  • Auditable Decision Trails: Every finding displays full evidence chains for independent peer review.
  • Objective Analytical Rigor: Deterministic computation combined with governed AI extraction models.
