Domain Applications

Engineered for High-Stakes Project Environments

When complex capital projects experience delay, scope changes, or contract disputes, PEIS transforms disorganized project record repositories into structured, verifiable evidence dossiers.

🔍
Core Evaluation

Complex Project Evaluation & Forensic Review

When a major infrastructure or commercial project concludes behind schedule or over budget, stakeholders face millions of pages of unstructured records. PEIS establishes an immutable chronology and maps every event back to governing contracts, isolating the root causes of project divergence.

Input Records

Contracts, specifications, all monthly schedule updates, meeting minutes, daily logs.

PEIS Analysis

As-Planned vs As-Built critical path evaluation, notice window auditing, and discrepancy detection.

Stakeholder Outcome

Comprehensive forensic report with clickable cryptographic citations to source pages.

🏗️
Field & Project Operations

Construction Management & General Contractor Oversight

Construction managers and general contractors manage rapid daily correspondence, RFIs, submittals, and inspection reports across dozens of subcontractors. PEIS continuously ingests field documentation to highlight open obligations and potential notice forfeitures in real time.

Input Records

RFI logs, submittal registries, daily inspector notes, subcontractor correspondence.

PEIS Analysis

RFI response duration tracking, delay event tagging, and daily report consistency checks.

Stakeholder Outcome

Early identification of bottleneck RFIs before they erode schedule float on the critical path.

📊
Schedule & Delay Analysis

Project Controls & Schedule Disruption Analysis

Project schedulers and controls managers must verify monthly CPM schedule updates submitted by contractors. PEIS performs deterministic schedule comparison to uncover hidden logic alterations, unapproved calendar changes, and artificial float suppression.

Input Records

Baseline and monthly Primavera XER/XML files, narrative schedule reports.

PEIS Analysis

Deterministic Schedule DNA comparison, float consumption auditing, critical path migration.

Stakeholder Outcome

Defensible, mathematical schedule integrity scorecard before monthly progress payment approval.

📋
Commercial Governance

Contract Administration & Milestone Compliance

Contract administrators must ensure strict adherence to notice provisions, deliverable submission windows, and milestone completion criteria across complex multi-prime agreements.

Input Records

Prime contract agreements, general conditions, formal notices, modification logs.

PEIS Analysis

Notice requirement mapping, timeliness calculation against triggering event dates, waiver risk flags.

Stakeholder Outcome

Clear compliance matrices protecting contractual rights and mitigating liability exposures.

⚖️
Dispute Resolution

Claims Analysis & Dispute Support

Forensic delay experts and legal teams preparing for mediation, arbitration, or Board of Contract Appeals proceedings require bulletproof factual substantiation. PEIS provides the factual substrate required to assemble or defend against delay and impact claims.

Input Records

Claim submittals, discovery document archives, emails, weather records, expert disclosures.

PEIS Analysis

Contradiction cross-matching, chronological event reconstruction, causal chain verification.

Stakeholder Outcome

Court-ready evidence mapping with zero ungrounded assertions and verifiable citations.

🏛️
Public Sector & Defense

Government Program Oversight & Audit Readiness

Federal, state, and municipal agencies managing capital programs must demonstrate transparent stewardship of public funds and defend against improper contractor claims. PEIS provides structured auditability aligned with government contracting standards (e.g., FAR Part 33 / Part 43).

Input Records

Government task orders, FAR clauses, contracting officer representative (COR) logs, certified claims.

PEIS Analysis

Statutory compliance mapping, certified claim entitlement auditing, contracting officer decision dossier prep.

Stakeholder Outcome

Total audit transparency protecting agency decisions against administrative appeals.

🎯
Executive Governance

Executive Project Decision Support & Portfolio Governance

Project sponsors, institutional owners, and C-level executives need concise, reliable factual summaries to approve major settlements, authorize budget augmentations, or negotiate disputed change orders.

Input Records

Executive project summaries, contractor position papers, risk scorecards, change order proposals.

PEIS Analysis

Synthesized risk scoring, commercial exposure quantification, and fact-grounded decision trade-offs.

Stakeholder Outcome

Defensible executive decision briefings with instant drill-down to supporting evidence.
