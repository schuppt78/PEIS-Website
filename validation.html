Transparent Boundaries

Documented Scope & Known Limitations

We believe analytical software credibility begins with a clear statement of operating limits.

1. Dependency on Input Record Completeness

PEIS evaluates evidence based strictly on ingested records. If critical project correspondence, unrecorded verbal agreements, or missing schedule updates are not provided to the system, evaluations will reflect the documentary record as ingested.

2. Non-Autonomous Decision Authority

PEIS is an analytical intelligence platform, not an autonomous arbiter, judge, or licensed professional engineer. All evaluations and findings must be reviewed and authorized by qualified professionals.

3. OCR Quality Constraints

While PEIS incorporates advanced optical character recognition for scanned records, severely degraded scans, handwritten field notes, or damaged historical plans may require human transcription assistance.
